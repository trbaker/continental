HOW TO PLAY

Take over the world — or at least 28 of its 40 territories. Each turn has three phases:

  • 1 · Reinforce. You get new armies: one for every 3 territories you hold (minimum 3), plus a bonus for each full continent. Tap your territories to place them.
  • 2 · Attack. Tap one of your territories with 2+ armies, then tap a neighboring enemy territory. Dice decide the battle: you roll up to 3 dice, the defender up to 2. Highest dice are compared — ties go to the defender. Blitz keeps rolling automatically.
  • 3 · Fortify. Move armies once between two of your neighboring territories, then end your turn.

Continent bonuses — N. America 3 · S. America 2 · Europe 5 · Africa 4 · Asia 7 · Oceania 2. Territories connected by sea routes (like Brazil ↔ Nigeria) count as neighbors.

Win by holding 28 territories or by being the last commander standing.
